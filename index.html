SidelineReel: confidence gate

AI tags youth-sports clips to each player's highlight reel, but look-alike jersey numbers (14→4, 12↔21) put clips in the wrong kid's reel. I designed a confidence gate: low-confidence clips go to the coach for a one-tap confirm, and anything unconfirmed is left out, never guessed.
